## Background

I was born in Hong Kong and moved to Canada for several years as a child, before returning to
Canada to study a BMath at the University of Waterloo. I then completed a Masters and PhD at the
City University of Hong Kong. In Dec 2021, I moved to the UK to work as a research associate at
the Institute for Manufacturing, University of Cambridge. I'm currently based in the Toronto area,
seeking industry roles in data science, simulation, or backend/data-focused software engineering.

My Erdős number is 3: Erdős &rarr; Charles K. Chui &rarr;
[Guanrong Chen](https://www.ee.cityu.edu.hk/~gchen/erdos.html) &rarr; me (thanks to
[this paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3032584)).

## Research interests

Most of my research and industry work sits in **discrete-event and stochastic simulation
modelling**, applied across a few different domains over the years:

- **Healthcare service delivery** &mdash; bed occupancy and patient flow modelling, including
  work on space-aware digital twins of hospital operations during my time at Cambridge
- **Telecommunications** &mdash; teletraffic theory and quality-of-service evaluation in mobile
  and overflow-loss network systems
- **Epidemiology** &mdash; compartmental (SEIHR-style) models of disease spread, including work
  on COVID-19 dynamics with migration effects
